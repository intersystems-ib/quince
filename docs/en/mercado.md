# Player market

[Español](../mercado.md) · [English](mercado.md) · [Français](../fr/mercado.md) · [Index](README.md)

- The engine maintains up to 20 generated agents and replaces the lowest-rated 20% weekly.
- Club-listed and released players do not consume those places; the market displays 20 players per page.
- Clubs set transfer fees and can withdraw listings. Players remain in their squads until signing.
- Buyers negotiate monthly wages and contracts for 1–5 seasons. Signing pays the transfer fee to the selling club.
- Automatic clubs list surplus players during reviews and recruit according to need and budget. Competing offers prioritise wages, then league position.

See [Transfer negotiations](FICHAJES.md).
