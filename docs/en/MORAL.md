# Morale across all clubs

[Español](../MORAL.md) · [English](MORAL.md) · [Français](../fr/MORAL.md) · [Index](README.md)

Morale ranges from 0 to 100. First teams, academies and generated agents in both divisions start at 100%.

A win restores the entire club squad to 100%, including unused replacements, unselected players and the academy. A defeat removes five points from everyone. Draws have no result-based morale effect. Knockout matches use the final winner, including a shoot-out. Played, simulated and friendly matches share these rules.

After fourteen days without playing minutes, a player loses one point per day. Time on the pitch resets that period; selection or sitting on the bench does not. A win restores morale, but an unused player can lose it again from the next day. Joining dates give new signings the same grace period. Academy players without matches follow the same rule.

Each rest or recovery day adds two morale points, capped at 100%, for every team and category. Inactivity loss is calculated separately, so the net gain can be one point. The summer break does not accumulate these gains.

Forwards can occupy any position from 1 to 8, and backs any position from 9 to 15, without losing morale. Switching between units temporarily deducts 15 points, or 3 for a secondary position. Returning to the same unit or leaving the XV restores base morale without stacking deductions. Match results affect base morale.
