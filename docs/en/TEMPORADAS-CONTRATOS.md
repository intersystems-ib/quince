# Seasons and contracts

[Español](../TEMPORADAS-CONTRATOS.md) · [English](TEMPORADAS-CONTRATOS.md) · [Français](../fr/TEMPORADAS-CONTRATOS.md) · [Index](README.md)

The season closes on 30 June and generates the next one, scheduling the cup before the league. The next advance skips to opening day on 1 September. Both events appear in the calendar and stop **Next event**. Results and history are retained.

The tenth-placed Honor regular-season club is relegated; the Élite play-off champion is promoted. Each league retains ten clubs and draws a new schedule.

Initial squad contracts expire across one to four seasons. Expired contracts make players free agents. Opponents review renewals as season-end approaches. Players can announce retirement from age 33, three months before competition ends; retiring players cannot renew.

Injuries, active contracts and multi-year obligations continue. July and August have no training or friendlies, but month-end payroll is settled during the skip. Generated academy players and free agents use the same quality scale as the rest of the players.
