# Competitions and fixtures

[Español](../COMPETICION.md) · [English](COMPETICION.md) · [Français](../fr/COMPETICION.md) · [Index](README.md)

Both divisions have ten teams, eighteen home-and-away rounds and ninety regular-season matches. The draw limits runs to two consecutive home or away fixtures.

The sporting season runs from the weekend nearest 27 September to the weekend nearest 7 June. The top four play semi-finals (1st–4th and 2nd–3rd) and a final. Each tie is a single match hosted by the higher regular-season finisher; pairings are filled as phases finish.

Standings award four points for a win, two for a draw, an attacking bonus for scoring three more tries than the opponent, and a defensive bonus for losing by seven points or fewer. Fixtures retain scores, tries and league points.

Competition has **Standings**, **Calendar** and **Statistics** tabs. Calendar marks wins green and defeats red and does not allow pending matches to be skipped. Manual friendlies offer three opponents. Deterministic automatic invitations arrive at most once a month for free weekends; they can be accepted or declined and expire after six days.

The bottom Honor regular-season club is relegated; the Élite play-off champion is promoted. See [Seasons](TEMPORADAS-CONTRATOS.md).

Copa del Rey includes twenty clubs from both divisions: six groups of three and two exempt teams. Group teams play once home and once away; the six group winners qualify. Quarter-finals, semi-finals and final are single matches. The 2026/27 edition follows the [FER groups and dates](https://ferugby.es/definidos-los-grupos-de-la-ntt-data-copa-de-s-m-el-rey-2026-27/); later editions use reproducible draws and nearby weekends. Honor and cup champions enter the next cup at the quarter-finals; a double winner gives the other direct place to the cup runner-up. Each season schedules the cup first, then the league on remaining free weekends.

Competition queries return compact fixtures with dates, scores, venues, standings points and match links. Internal simulation details remain stored and are not repeated in the calendar, keeping the response small throughout the season.
