# Injuries

[Español](../LESIONES.md) · [English](LESIONES.md) · [Français](../fr/LESIONES.md) · [Index](README.md)

Injuries occur in matches and training, influenced by age, fatigue, intensity and conditioning. Base match risk is 0.000045 per player per twenty seconds. These are gameplay parameters.

| Injury | Grade | Weeks |
|---|---|---|
| Bruising | 1 / 2 | 1 / 1–2 |
| Muscle overload | 1 | 1–2 |
| Muscle tightness | 1 | 1–2 |
| Ankle sprain | 1 / 2 / 3 | 1–2 / 3–5 / 8–12 |
| Muscle fibre injury | 1 / 2 | 1–2 / 3–6 |
| Muscle tear | 3 | 10–16 |
| Adductor injury | 2 | 2–4 |
| Hamstring injury | 2 | 3–6 |
| Shoulder sprain | 2 | 2–4 |
| Shoulder dislocation | 3 | 6–10 |
| Concussion | 2 | 2–4 |
| Meniscus injury | 3 | 6–10 |
| Collarbone fracture | 3 | 8–12 |
| Ankle fracture | 3 | 12–18 |
| Cruciate ligament tear | 3 | 28–40 |
| Achilles tendon rupture | 3 | 24–36 |

A red cross identifies injuries. Injured replacements automatically move to **Unavailable**; starters must be replaced. Recovery retains history and returns the player to the bench if fewer than eight replacements are selected, otherwise to **Not selected**. A player still listed as a starter keeps that place.

During absence, players recover energy without attribute training. An injury in your team pauses the match; choose a speed to resume. During play only the injured player is identified; diagnosis and duration are available in profiles and management screens. Opponents share the same injury and availability rules.
