# Cards and exclusions

[Español](../DISCIPLINA.md) · [English](DISCIPLINA.md) · [Français](../fr/DISCIPLINA.md) · [Index](README.md)

A yellow card excludes a player for ten minutes of playing time. A second yellow or a red card means permanent dismissal. Excluded players disappear from the pitch and actions; numerical disadvantage affects the engine.

An eligible player returns when the temporary sanction ends. Half-time and pauses do not consume sin-bin time. The state survives reloads and restarts.

Commentary, the scoreboard and player lists show cards. Red cards are permanent: no twenty-minute replacement and no accumulated suspensions between matches are modelled.
