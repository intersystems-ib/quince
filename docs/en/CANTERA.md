# Academy and contracts

[Español](../CANTERA.md) · [English](CANTERA.md) · [Français](../fr/CANTERA.md) · [Index](README.md)

Squad and Training separate first team and academy with tabs. Each club maintains at least twenty youth players, with low wages and position coverage.

The academy coach determines improvement in the trained attribute. Young players accumulate fatigue and injuries like seniors. Newly generated Spanish players have two surnames.

To promote a player, negotiate the wage and sign. Identity and history are retained when he joins the first team; there is no signing bonus. Both categories allow contract termination with confirmation and the displayed compensation; the player becomes a free agent.

At season closing, every club, including the user's, automatically receives five or six academy players with varied averages based on academy coach quality.
