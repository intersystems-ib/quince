# Opposition preparation

[Español](../PREPARACION-RIVAL.md) · [English](PREPARACION-RIVAL.md) · [Français](../fr/PREPARACION-RIVAL.md) · [Index](README.md)

Every official league or cup fixture with both teams assigned has a meeting two days beforehand, shown in Calendar and **This week's plan** on Your club. The Coaching staff **Opposition preparation** tab opens the report from that date through matchday inclusive, before kick-off. The preparation-in-progress message starts the day after the previous match, including friendlies; for the first fixture, at season opening.

The report compares the selected XV with a probable opposition line-up in set pieces, contact, open attack, defence and kicking. Forwards and backs are analysed separately; energy and morale affect projected performance. Staff quality determines estimation accuracy. Reports reveal neither confirmed opposition selection nor tactics.

Analysis combines comparison, strengths, risks and a recommendation covering build-up, tempo, defensive pressure and penalty priority. **Apply recommendation** saves tactics to the current match squad without changing attributes, energy, morale or training.

**Update report** recalculates using the current squad. If the report or line-up version changes, update before applying. Friendlies and ties without assigned teams generate no meeting.

**Next event** advances one day at a time until a meeting or match, including friendlies. A meeting opens its Coaching staff tab; matchday offers **Go to match**. Stopping finishes after the current day is saved. Unsaved training or line-up changes block starting or continuing automatic advance. Each day runs training, finances and opponent management with its own audit.
