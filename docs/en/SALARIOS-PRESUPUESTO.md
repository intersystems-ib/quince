# Salaries and budgets

[Español](../SALARIOS-PRESUPUESTO.md) · [English](SALARIOS-PRESUPUESTO.md) · [Français](../fr/SALARIOS-PRESUPUESTO.md) · [Index](README.md)

Salaries and offers are monthly. Payroll is paid at month end and prorated by days.

| Group | Monthly reference |
|---|---:|
| Top Honor | €850–2,400 |
| Mid-table Honor | €800–2,000 |
| Lower Honor | €450–1,400 |
| Elite | €300–1,100 |
| Academy | €30–100 |

Wages vary with quality and sporting headroom. Human managers can hire staff without a wage cap; AI clubs check affordability. Training adds no daily charge.

Whole-club budgets include membership, season tickets, sponsors, grants, gate receipts, bar concessions and other activities. Youth and rugby school fees have separate operating costs outside the managed squads. Cisneros and Alcobendas use a €600,000 whole-club reference. Full initial budgets leave around 2% with a reference-quality financial director; recruitment decisions and results may change the margin.

Opening cash covers one payroll, one month of grassroots activity, registrations and one month of travel, plus a 10% buffer. It applies to new or untouched opening-day saves. Advanced saves retain their cash and historical receipts and expenses.

**Reinvest surplus in transfers** allocates up to 90% of additional headroom to wages, after whole-club costs and excluding pending grants. Fee-free renewals at the same or a lower wage remain possible over the cap.
