# Finances

[Español](../FINANZAS.md) · [English](FINANZAS.md) · [Français](../fr/FINANZAS.md) · [Index](README.md)

The user hires a finance director, who manages September income according to quality and the game seed.

| Date | Activity |
|---|---|
| 7 September | Main sponsor: €30,000–80,000; five secondary sponsors: €1,000–5,000 each |
| 14 September | Grants from generic local public bodies |
| 21 September | Season-ticket campaign |
| 28 September | Bar concession: €25,000–45,000 annually |

Sponsorship and bar agreements last one to three seasons, paid monthly from September to June. The concession operator covers bar costs. Generated names avoid duplicates.

Grant shares vary by club, season and seed; the town council contributes a larger proportion. Allocation preserves the total awarded.

Grants cover half the amount calculated by the funding model, with variation based on the finance director. Awarded grants do not grow with every signing or automatically cover deficits. Forecasts reflect reduced income. Adjustments to existing agreements retain received payments and reduce outstanding amounts.

Gate receipts depend on ticket price, attendance and season-ticket holders, capped by capacity. Initial prices consider division, stage, opponent position, derbies and decisive late-season matches. Standings are used after three matches; before then, club reference quality is used. Recommendations range from €5 to €40; the host may set €0–50 before kick-off. Friendlies split gate receipts equally.

**Ground and tickets** shows name, capacity and a generic illustration for up to 1,000; 1,001–2,500; 2,501–5,000; 5,001–10,000; or more than 10,000 spectators. These are illustrative images, not photographs of the actual grounds.
