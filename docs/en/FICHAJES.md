# Transfers

[Español](../FICHAJES.md) · [English](FICHAJES.md) · [Français](../fr/FICHAJES.md) · [Index](README.md)

The shared market includes free agents and club-listed players, with twenty players per page. The twenty-candidate cap applies only to engine-generated agents; club listings and released players do not use those places. Every club can buy and list players.

Sellers set fees and can withdraw listings. Players stay at their clubs until the buyer agrees a contract and signs; the fee then transfers to the seller. Every seven game days, the lowest-rated 20% of generated agents leave, rounded to whole players, and foreign players replenish that pool to twenty. Club listings are excluded from this rotation.

Players leaving the generated pool retain profiles and history. Open negotiations expire and those players become unavailable for signing. Reading the market or retrying an advance generates no extra candidates.

Negotiate monthly wages and a 1–5-year contract, review counteroffers and sign. Players up to 24 prefer 2–3 seasons; ages 25–32 prefer five; from 33, one. Other terms require a wage premium. **Accept counteroffer** agrees exactly the offered wage and duration. Negotiations allow five offers and last seven days. Signing pays the fee once and validates availability, funds and wage limits. Promotions and renewals also require signing.

Offers compete on monthly wages, then league position; Honor ranks ahead of Élite. An agreed human offer participates on the same terms but is never signed automatically.

Long injuries permit daily cover recruitment and a limited wage reserve. See [Wages](SALARIOS-PRESUPUESTO.md). Generated agents have foreign names and nationalities, position-appropriate attributes and the same quality scale as Spanish clubs. Generated Spanish initial-squad and academy players have two surnames.

Renewals at the same or lower monthly wage, without additional payments, can be signed even above the wage limit. Increases, promotions and new signings retain their validations.
