# Match engine

[Español](../MOTOR-PARTIDOS.md) · [English](MOTOR-PARTIDOS.md) · [Français](../fr/MOTOR-PARTIDOS.md) · [Index](README.md)

Actions use the relevant participants: forwards at scrums, mauls and rucks; receivers, passers, kickers and defenders in open play. Their attributes, energy, morale and tactics matter. Switching positions within the same unit does not reduce morale or attributes; moving between forwards and backs can temporarily reduce morale.

The match retains its seed, clock, ball, coordinates, statistics and commands. Players move according to phase and possession; animation interpolates received states.

All speeds share the same sporting rules. Pause is manual or automatic at half-time and for your team's injuries; selecting a speed resumes play. Substitutions take place with the ball dead and are revalidated before execution.

See [Rules](REGLAS.md), [Tactics](TACTICAS.md), [Cards](DISCIPLINA.md) and [Injuries](LESIONES.md).

Before kickoff, choose **Watch** for the live match with speed controls, or **Result**. Result simulates the first half on start, stops at half-time for substitutions and tactics, then completes the second half with **Go to fulltime**. Both modes use the same engine, including injuries, cards, added time, statistics and financial settlement; Result has no intermediate pauses. The mode is fixed at kickoff and survives reloads.
