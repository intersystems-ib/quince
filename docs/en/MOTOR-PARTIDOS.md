# Match engine

[Español](../MOTOR-PARTIDOS.md) · [English](MOTOR-PARTIDOS.md) · [Français](../fr/MOTOR-PARTIDOS.md) · [Index](README.md)

Actions use the relevant participants: forwards at scrums, mauls and rucks; receivers, passers, kickers and defenders in open play. Their attributes, energy, morale and tactics matter. Playing out of position reduces morale, not attributes.

The match retains its seed, clock, ball, coordinates, statistics and commands. Players move according to phase and possession; animation interpolates received states.

All speeds share the same sporting rules. Pause is manual or automatic at half-time and for your team's injuries; selecting a speed resumes play. Substitutions take place with the ball dead and are revalidated before execution.
