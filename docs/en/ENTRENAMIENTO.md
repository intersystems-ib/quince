# Training and coaching staff

[Español](../ENTRENAMIENTO.md) · [English](ENTRENAMIENTO.md) · [Français](../fr/ENTRENAMIENTO.md) · [Index](README.md)

The first team uses a recurring weekly plan. Choose an overall focus and select **Request plan** to generate a proposal. Seven day cards let you adjust the session and separate forwards and backs drills. Save to apply changes.

Academy training is automatic, including existing games. The academy coach manages a cycle of skills, fitness, attack, defence, set pieces, tactics and rest; improvement depends exclusively on that coach's quality.

Staff include new players, reduce intensity for age or fatigue and prioritise recovery for unavailable players or energy below 60%. The first team also recovers on matchday and the day before.

**Squad status and weekly load** shows the next opponent, availability by unit, indicative plan load and players unavailable, below 75% energy or below 70% morale. Its recommendation can add recovery to the first-team draft. Load summarises sessions and rest; it does not predict future energy.

Seventeen attributes range from 1 to 100 with internal decimals. Rest restores energy and is not an attribute. Academy training uses its coach; the first team combines head, specialist and support coaches at 15%, 65% and 20%.

| Age | Target average change per season |
|---|---:|
| Up to 24 | +6 to +8 |
| 25–28 | +2 to +5 |
| 29–32 | 0 to +2 |
| 33–35 | −4 to −2 |
| From 36 | −7 to −5 |

Coach quality weights the range. Each session improves only the chosen attribute: average gain per session × 17, adjusted for intensity. Decline applies daily. Attribute bounds and rest can reduce effective progression.

There are eight roles: head, forwards, backs, defence, attack, skills, fitness and academy. Each has quality and monthly wages. Sessions have no additional charge.

Profiles show current average and end-of-season potential based on the active plan. Each attribute shows its season change in green or red, using integers while retaining fractions internally. See [Wages](SALARIOS-PRESUPUESTO.md).

The staff market keeps three candidates per role. Hiring generates a replacement; dismissed employees leave the market, while their contracts retain history. Reading the candidate list generates nobody.
