# Grounds and capacities

[Español](../CAMPOS.md) · [English](CAMPOS.md) · [Français](../fr/CAMPOS.md) · [Index](README.md)

Published 2026/27 references, accessed on 10 November 2026: [Honor](https://es.wikipedia.org/wiki/Divisi%C3%B3n_de_Honor_de_rugby_2026-27) and [Élite](https://es.wikipedia.org/wiki/Divisi%C3%B3n_de_Honor_%C3%89lite_de_rugby_2026-27). `../../iris/data/venues.json` retains ground name, capacity, season, access date and source for all twenty clubs. Capacities come from those publications, not an administrative capacity verification.

Clubs keep their ground when changing divisions. Each fixture stores the home club's ground; older records without one use home-club data. Ground information appears in the club centre, directory, calendar, competition, match preview and scoreboard. Missing data is shown as unavailable.
